Next time an urge shows up, ask yourself four questions: Am I hungry? Angry? Lonely? Tired?

It's called HALT, and it's used in recovery programs for a reason. Very often the urge isn't about what it seems to be about. It's your body asking for food, rest, connection or a way to let off steam.

Eat something. Call someone. Go to bed. Then see if the urge is still there. 🍃

The simplest trick is also one of the strongest: move.

In a review of 12 studies on smokers, a single short session of exercise reduced cravings in every one of them. Porn cravings run on the same reward system, so the same move is worth trying. You don't need a gym. A brisk walk, some push-ups or a few flights of stairs will do.

Get up, get out of the room, and let your body reset. 🏃

(Source: Taylor, Ussher & Faulkner, Addiction, 2007)
